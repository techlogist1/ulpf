Both files share one wire-format trait not in `samples/cisco_asa.log`: every message is prefixed
`<hostname> :<RFC3339-timestamp>: %ASA-...` (an empty device-id before the ` : ` separator, then
an ISO-8601 timestamp, then `: %ASA-...`) — the `logging timestamp rfc5424` variant the parser's
own header comment names but that none of its three `[strategy].patterns` actually match against
(see validation below: **100% of both real files fail with `pattern_no_match`**).
